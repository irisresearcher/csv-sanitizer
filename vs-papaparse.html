Technical Summary: Why PapaParse Breaks & How Shield Heals It

When an unescaped double quote appears inside a field (e.g. 2,"Bob "The Builder" Smith",bob@example.com), PapaParse interprets the internal quote as the end of the cell. The subsequent space or comma is interpreted as a boundary, shifting the subsequent text into the wrong column or throwing an Undetected closing quote syntax warning.

CSV Shield implements a streaming lookahead tokenizer that inspects following characters. If a quote does not touch a cell boundary or delimiter, it is recognized as mid-field punctuation and automatically transformed into standard RFC 4180 double quotes (""), preserving 100% of data columns in their expected positions.
