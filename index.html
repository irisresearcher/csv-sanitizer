Frequently Asked Questions

How does CSV Shield fix broken quotes without uploading my data?

CSV Shield executes entirely within your browser's local JavaScript engine and Web Workers. It analyzes raw text lines using RFC 4180 heuristic regex matching to identify internal quotes in cells without ever transmitting data over the Internet.

Why does Microsoft Excel shift my CSV data into wrong rows?

When a CSV field contains an unescaped double quote, standard spreadsheet parsers interpret it as the start of an open multiline block. This shifts the rest of the row into subsequent cells. CSV Shield identifies and escapes internal quotes automatically.

Is CSV Shield safe for confidential and GDPR-protected data?

Yes. CSV Shield has zero cloud backends, zero analytics beacons, and zero telemetry. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane Mode and the app continues working completely offline.

How do I resolve mangled accents and characters (mojibake)?

Mangled characters occur when legacy systems export Windows-1252 files opened as UTF-8. CSV Shield contains a multi-byte heuristic table that automatically repairs common corrupt sequences and prepends an Excel-compatible UTF-8 BOM.
