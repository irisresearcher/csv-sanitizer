📌 1. Project Overview & Problem Statement

Every single day, millions of accountants, business analysts, e-commerce sellers, and software engineers download data exports from platforms like Shopify, Salesforce, Jira, banking portals, or legacy ERP databases. Almost always, spreadsheets fail to open properly due to small, pervasive formatting corruptions:

  • Unescaped Internal Quotes: Inch marks (24" Display) or HTML product descriptions cause parsers to fail with "Illegal quoting on line X" or throw columns out of alignment.
  • Multiline Field Corruption: When addresses or descriptions contain newlines, naive tools chop the row into pieces.
  • Excel Leading Zero Destruction: Microsoft Excel automatically deletes leading zeros from SKUs, ZIP codes, and bank accounts (e.g. 008492 becomes 8492).
  • Character Mojibake: Accented names and European currency symbols become garbled (e.g. Café turns into CafÃ©, € becomes â‚¬).
  • Corporate Privacy & Security Breaches: Frustrated employees upload internal sales spreadsheets to random online conversion websites, directly violating corporate Data Loss Prevention (DLP), GDPR, and HIPAA compliance policies.

🧠 3. Technical Architecture & Engineering Innovations

The core engine (engine.js) was engineered with zero external dependencies (no npm packages, no heavy frameworks) for maximum speed, security, and portable longevity.

1. Streaming State Machine Tokenizer:

Processes raw character streams sequentially without naively splitting on \n. Handles multi-line quotes, escaped quotes (""), and backslash-escaped quotes (\") in a single pass.

2. Heuristic Lookahead Healer (isPseudoClosingQuote):

Solves ambiguous quotes (e.g. "He said "Hello", then walked away",active). Looks ahead to the next token; if the subsequent token ends with a quote without starting with one, the algorithm recognizes the quote as internal dialogue rather than a column boundary, preventing catastrophic column shifts.

3. Statistical Delimiter Detection:

Calculates variance scores across commas (,), semicolons (;), tabs (\t), and pipes (|) across sample lines, ensuring 100% correct delimiter discovery even on European files.

4. Live Zero-Network Traffic Proof:

Users can click "Inspect" to view real-time performance instrumentation proving 0 HTTP/WebSocket transmissions have taken place.
